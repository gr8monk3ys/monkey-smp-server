# Surface brief: the Monkey SMP landing page

Scope: `site/index.html` (mode: Persuade). `site/changes.html` (Read) and `site/creators.html` (Read) share
`site/style.css` and inherit this world.

Audience and job: Minecraft Java players arriving from creator videos, TikTok and server lists, often on a
phone, deciding whether to join (newcomers first); members coming back for the address, the pack and the rules.
Action: copy the address, get a pass (the Discord until the store opens), join the Discord.
Proof on hand: six real screenshots, the one-minute trailer (staged scenes, labelled), the logo kit.
Constraints: every fact, price, rule and link stays as written; `data-link`, `data-store`, `data-launch` and
the ids app.js reads stay; no invented players, counts or quotes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the monkey's own jungle drawn in Minecraft's flat block language, and the server's hard
numbers are its structure. It refuses the category default: a dark page over a blurred screenshot, a glowing
copy-IP button and a row of cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep jungle-leaf green owns most of the page; bone and gold come from the wordmark;
cobblestone grey is the stone for reference surfaces (address plaque, rule tablets, moss along the tablets'
tops). Every object is flat block colour with a stepped bevel, a 2px outline (ink, except the bone ghost button
and the leaf outline of the optional Supporter tier) and stepped pixel corners, as the logo is drawn: no cast shadows, glows,
gradients, blur or pills. Leaf-block canopy edges crown a few sections only. A blocky display face in the
wordmark's spirit; a hyperlegible body face. Colour code: gold is action, bone is text, stone is reference,
leaf is ground.

STORY: The visitor sees the jungle and the monkey, reads the hook, sees Season I's date ticking down, copies the
address or gets a pass. Scrolling shows the real world (each screenshot tagged with where it was taken), three
steps in, one fair price, the rules carved as tablets, the FAQ. Members find address, pack and rules in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A canopy edge across the top; nav row with mark and wordmark left, section links right; the
hook large in bone, left-weighted; Season I's date with the countdown as gold digits on shade blocks (days, hours,
minutes); the address on a stone plaque with Copy; Get a pass as the gold block button beside Join the
Discord in bone outline. The trailer starts at the fold.

FORM: Canopy, candidate 7 of 7 (the brand's literal world, made to work), seed: none (unavailable).
Raises: numbers as structure (from the nixie counter); every screenshot carries a tag of where it was taken
(from the moon-shadow bazaar); no cast shadow anywhere, every object outlined (from the reverse-perspective
bookshelf); a strict colour code (from the Factory sleeve); one quiet field per section so the action owns it
(from the accretion disk). Signature interaction: countdown digits step like blocks (steps() timing), the Copy
button presses down 2px (one pixel of the frame art). Motion is stepped, never gliding; reduced motion is still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
