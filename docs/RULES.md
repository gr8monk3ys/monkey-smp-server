# Monkey SMP: rules

The full rulebook, for `#smp-rules` in the Discord. The website (`site/index.html`, "Rules") shows the
short version; when a rule changes, change both. Draft until the owners approve it.

**Short version:** fight hard, play fair, don't be cruel. Staff decide edge cases, and you can appeal any
decision.

## In game

### 1. Respect people
- No slurs, hate speech, harassment, threats, or sharing anyone's personal information (doxxing), in game,
  in voice chat or in the Discord.
- Trash talk about the game is fine: your base, your faction, your fighting. Going after the person is not:
  their race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or real life.
- If someone asks you to stop messaging them outside the game, stop.

### 2. No cheating
- No hacked clients, x-ray of any kind (mods, resource packs, glitches), combat macros, auto-clickers,
  kill aura, reach, fly or speed hacks, or entity radar and ESP.
- Play with the modpack. Shaders, performance mods (Sodium, ImmediatelyFast and the like), and cosmetic
  client mods are fine. The minimap and world map that come with the pack are fine.
- Not sure about a mod? Ask in a ticket before you use it, not after.

### 3. Report exploits, don't use them
- No duplication glitches, lag machines, chunk-loading tricks, or abusing a mod bug to get items, strength
  or land.
- Report bugs in a private ticket, not in public chat. A real find earns the Bughunter title.
- Using an exploit you didn't report counts as cheating.

### 4. Claims are the line
- Inside a claim, the owner's settings decide who can build, open chests and use things.
- Outside claims, everything is contested: PvP, looting, and taking apart unclaimed builds are part of the
  game. Claim what you want to keep.
- Don't claim land just to wall in someone else's base, block the only way out of it, or shut players out
  of spawn, the end portal, or a structure everyone needs.
- Claim names, party names and banners follow rule 9.

### 5. Fight fair
- No PvP at spawn or within 48 blocks of it.
- Don't log out, or use `/home` or other teleports, to escape a fight you're in.
- Don't camp someone's respawn. After you kill a player, leave them alone for five minutes.
- No traps at spawn or along the spawn road that kill players who haven't left yet.

### 6. Strength is earned
- Strength comes from real fights. No "feeding": killing a friend, an ally or your own alt over and over to
  move strength around.
- Don't trade kills for strength, and don't let someone kill you on purpose for strength in exchange for
  items or favors.

### 7. One account each
- No alternate accounts to dodge a ban, stretch claim limits, feed your own strength, or hold land while
  you're offline.
- Two people sharing a house and a connection is fine: tell staff in a ticket so the shared IP isn't
  flagged.

### 8. No real-money trading
- Don't sell or buy items, land, strength, ranks or accounts for real money, gift cards, crypto, or items
  in other games.
- In-game deals between players aren't enforced by staff. Trade carefully. Scamming someone out of real
  money is a ban.

### 9. Keep it all-ages
- Names, skins, signs, books, banners, builds and chat stay free of sexual, hateful or gory content.
- No advertising other servers or Discords.

### 10. Keep the server fast
- Farms are welcome. Giant ones that drag everyone's tick rate down are not: keep mob farms to one per
  party, and turn redstone clocks off when you leave.
- If a build causes lag, staff will talk to you first, and only change it if it can't be fixed together.
- Don't fly or boat across huge distances at high speed just to load new land; the world is pre-generated
  inside the border for a reason.

## In the Discord

1. Rules 1, 8 and 9 apply here too.
2. Use the right place: questions before buying in `#smp-faq`; suggestions, bugs, recruitment and trading
   in `#smp-forum` with the right tag; payment problems, reports and appeals in a ticket.
3. No spam, mass pings, or pinging staff for something a ticket can handle.
4. No DMs to staff about bans or reports. Use a ticket so there's a record and anyone on staff can help.
5. Recruitment and trading posts go in the forum, not in chat.
6. Don't post someone else's messages, IP or personal details, and don't post clips to shame a player.
   Clips of good fights are welcome.

## How rules are enforced

- **Warning**, then a **temporary ban** (1 to 7 days), then a **permanent ban**.
- Cheating, exploits used for gain, hate speech, doxxing and real-money scams can skip straight to a ban.
- Staff can roll back items, strength or land gained by breaking a rule, and can remove builds or claims
  that break rule 4 or rule 9.
- Staff decide edge cases. A rule not being listed doesn't make something allowed if it ruins the game for
  others.

## Appeals

- Open a ticket in the Discord (Front Desk) and say what happened. One appeal per decision.
- A different staff member than the one who acted reviews it, within 7 days.
- Lying in an appeal ends it.

## Payments and bans

- The Server Pass is the same price for everyone. Supporter tiers are cosmetic and give no advantage.
- A ban doesn't refund time already paid for. Cancel your pass in the store if you don't want it to renew.
- If you can't get the modpack running in your first 48 hours, open a ticket for a refund.
- Chargebacks on a pass are a permanent ban; open a ticket instead and we'll sort it out.
