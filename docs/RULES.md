# Monkey SMP: Season I rules

The full rulebook, for `#smp-rules` in the Discord. The website (`site/index.html`, "Rules") shows the
short version; when a rule changes, change both. Rule numbers follow the owners' rulebook, so "rule 35"
means the same thing everywhere.

Hosted by Diego A. Scaturchio and Lorenzo D. Scaturchio.

Welcome everyone! This is our first attempt at an open, non-anarchy PvP server. We're using the Season 4
version of the original Strength SMP plugin from YouTube. In this SMP you'll face alliances, factions,
enemies, wars, and events that change the course of the game. Below are the rules and bans that keep the
server safe and fun. If something turns out to be unfun or unfair, the rules can be adjusted based on
player feedback.

Since it's a modpack, a section points out useful mods every player should know about. All the mods are
vanilla+ quality-of-life mods that support the gameplay and performance. We hope you all enjoy the server!

## Bases & Combat

1. No private bases.
   - All bases must have one notable feature that can be seen from the surface.
2. You are engaged in combat once a player has successfully **attacked** you.
   - While engaged, all combat rules and bans must be followed.
3. No combat logging.
   - That means logging out during a fight, or 3 minutes into a chase (such as a mole hunt).
4. No respawn or logout trapping.
5. No farming players or naked killing.
   - A naked player is one who has freshly respawned and has nothing on them (seeds and filler items
     don't count).
   - Naked players trying to retrieve loot can be killed if they are actively trying to steal your loot.
6. No hovering in fights as a naked player. If you do, you can be slain.
7. No loopholes.
   - If you have a question about whether something is legit, contact an admin to confirm before you do it.
8. No griefing spawn, unless you're griefing your own builds or the affected party is OK with it.
   - The admin spawn building is not to be touched unless warranted.
9. No returning to team fights after death.
   - 10+ minutes must pass after a team fight has ended to re-engage.
10. If a player streams, no stream sniping unless you have the streamer's permission.
11. No restocking during fights:
    - from an ender chest,
    - from a base,
    - from a shulker box.
12. No using leads on dolphins during fights.
13. No AFK killing.
14. No killing players who are obviously lagging out mid-fight or have a hardware problem.
15. No elytra-ing directly into a fight: land at least 50 blocks away.
16. No elytra in combat or during combat.
17. No Happy Ghast PvP:
    - spearing,
    - macing,
    - bowing or crossbowing,
    - sulfur bombing.
    - To join or engage a fight, the player must dismount from the ghast, out of the air.
18. No lava PvP in fights. This is absolute, whether you plan to use it on yourself or on others.
19. No water running:
    - A player who retreats into the ocean can be at most ~30 blocks from the closest shore. Players
      beyond that limit must actively try to get back within that radius or reach shore.
    - No boating endlessly in the ocean.
    - No swimming in circles endlessly to avoid combat.
20. No stealing or killing pets while their owner is offline, in the owner's territory:
    dogs, cats, parrots, horses and Happy Ghasts (renamed mobs included).
21. No account sharing.

## World rules and restrictions

22. Spawn is sacred and can't be tampered with by anyone.
23. For the first hour after launch, PvP, the Nether and the End are closed.
    - When the hour is up, PvP and **only** the Nether open.
24. Until notified, players **can't** craft or use Netherite armor, but **can** use Netherite tools and
    weapons.
25. A world border is set at 2500 x 2500.
    - Leaving or breaking the world border is prohibited.
    - The border is expected to grow as the server progresses or as more players join.
26. New players and spawn are safe from PvP.
    - For 48 hours after your first join, other players can't hurt you. Attacking a player ends that early,
      and so does `/trigger end_grace`. Nobody gets it in the launch hour (rule 23): the whole server starts
      together.
    - Grace is for settling in. Using it to scout, carry loot, block a fight, hold a spot for a faction, or
      fight by other means (pets, lava, fire, traps) is a loophole (rule 7), and an admin will end it.
    - Spawn is a no-PvP zone.

## Strength rules

27. No ender-chesting Strength while you're below +5 Strength.
28. Strength can only be crafted up to +3, and crafting can't take you past that.
    - Strength can be shared among players in a faction until a player hits +5. After that, that player
      can't be given more Strength to hold in their ender chest.
    - Strength can be traded with other factions or non-faction players as many times as you like.
    - All Strength you receive must be used immediately, unless you're at +5.

## Factions

29. Factions can have at most 4 players at a time.
30. All factions are public and must have a banner that represents them.
31. Alliances are allowed; confederations are not.
32. If a group of more than 4 players keeps working together, again and again, every player in it can be
    reported and lose their Strength for breaking the faction rules.
33. Once you join a faction, you're in it until the server ends.
    - Leaving a faction needs an admin's approval.
34. No starting KOS inside an enemy player's base or territory.
35. You may start KOS in your own territory, which starts a fight.
    - Territory is 32 x 32 blocks (2 x 2 chunks) per player, and must be marked with a banner representing
      you or your faction.
    - A faction's territory grows with each member: 2 x 2 chunks per person.
    - Anything outside that perimeter can be griefed.
36. A territory is either the inside of a base **or** an area enclosed by a wall around its perimeter.
    - Walls must be at least 3 blocks tall.
    - The inside of a base means the literal inside of a house or building.
37. No one may grief a territory without permission.
    - This doesn't apply to a player in the territory who is disarming or escaping a trap.
    - Pranks on a base must be fully reversible by whoever did the prank.
    - Griefing a base, getting caught, and not having owned up to it is bannable.
38. If you retreat into your own territory while engaged in combat, you still have to fight.
    - Blocks and builds there still fall under the anti-griefing and combat-rejoining rules.
39. No territory can be griefed, robbed, sabotaged, abused or exploited, or farmed.
40. No insiding: griefing, raiding or robbing your own faction, or helping someone else do it.
    - Planning one counts too, when there's evidence it was going to happen.
    - After you leave a faction or are removed from it, you can't use anything you had as a member against
      it: access to its base, chests or claims, its coordinates, an alt left inside, or members who still
      think you're on their side.
    - Doing it through an alt or another player is punished as if you did it yourself.
    - Fake-checking a faction's walls or traps (saying they're fine when they aren't) is insiding.
    - First offense: a 14-day ban. Second: a permanent ban. A faction that took part or profited gives back
      what it took, and its members can be punished too.
41. No teaming: don't help anyone outside your faction in a fight.
    - When other factions are fighting, or one is raiding another, stay out: don't attack either side,
      block them, heal them or take the loot.
    - Events are exempt. At KOTH, in the arena and at other staff events anyone can fight anyone; the rule
      applies again once you leave the event area.
    - First offense: a 2-day ban. Second: 5 days. Third: 14 days. Offenses count for the current season.
42. Admins roll back griefing of a territory (rules 37 and 39) and an insider's damage (rule 40). A legal
    raid, on anything outside a territory's perimeter, isn't rolled back.

## Trading

43. Trade by hand, items for items: there is no money on the server.
    - No scams. Give what you said you'd give: no ads or deals in chat that lie about the item, the amount or
      the price, no changing a deal after the other player agreed to it, and no items renamed to look like
      something they aren't. A scammer gives back what they took, and repeat scams are bannable.
    - Items only. Buying or selling for real money is still banned ("No real-money trading" below).

## Bedrock

A netherite pickaxe breaks bedrock, slowly (about 5 seconds with Efficiency V), and the bedrock doesn't drop.

44. No holes into the void. Don't break the bottom layer of bedrock in any dimension, and no traps that drop
    players or their things out of the world.
45. No breaking the bedrock of the End's exit portal or of an End gateway.
46. No going above the Nether roof: no building, travelling or hiding on top of it.

## Bans

These are banned on the server.

- **B1.** Strength can't be brewed, put on tipped arrows, or applied to a player.
- **B2.** Enchanted maces of any kind.
- **B3.** Ender pearls, including for fun. They're only for crafting.
- **B4.** Totems of Undying, including for fun.
- **B5.** Enchanted golden apples, including for fun. They're only for crafting.
- **B6.** Debuffs, unless for personal use (on yourself). A debuff is any effect shown in red in the JEI screen.
  - Exception: with a **bow or crossbow**, these are allowed: Slowness I, Weakness I, Poison I,
    Instant Harming I and Turtle Master.
- **B7.** Crystal PvP, including for fun.
- **B8.** Bed bombing, including for fun.
- **B9.** Fire Aspect and Flame enchantments.
- **B10.** TNT minecarts and cart traps. Carts can still be used to set off a trap.
- **B11.** TNT traps at spawn, unless the owner approves.
- **B12.** Escape tunnels: tunnels that run long distances to escape or disengage from combat. Trap
  tunnels are fine.
- **B13.** Any exploit or glitch that gives a competitive edge.
- **B14.** The view-bobbing glitch.
- **B15.** Unfair clients, mods, texture packs or glitches: hacking, duping, CIT packs, x-ray, health
  indicators and the like. Anything else must be approved by the server owners.
- **B16.** Replay Mod or Flashback for a competitive advantage. Videos and build photos are fine.
- **B17.** The cushion glitch (an x-ray glitch).
- **B18.** Enchantment books from the Nova Structures mod are only server event rewards. Books found
  naturally can't be applied.
- **B19.** F3+A and reloading shaders. These count as x-ray.

## The mods

Coming soon: the mods every player should know about. New in 2.8.0, and worth knowing now:

- **Carry On.** Sneak and right-click with both hands empty to pick up a furnace, barrel or spawner with what's
  in it, or a passive animal, and right-click to put it down. Not chests, banners, beds, doors, players or the
  NPCs at spawn, and nothing in someone else's territory.
- **Loot chests refill.** A structure's loot chest that you empty fills again a week later. Once one has been
  opened, only staff can break it.
- **Bedrock breaks** with a netherite pickaxe (rules 44 to 46).

## Community and payments

These carry over from the server's earlier rulebook.

- **Respect people.** No slurs, hate speech, harassment, threats or sharing anyone's personal
  information, in game, in voice chat or in the Discord. Trash talk about the game is fine; going after the
  person is not.
- **No real-money trading.** Don't buy or sell items, land, Strength or accounts for real money, gift
  cards or crypto.
- **Keep it all-ages.** Names, skins, signs, banners, builds and chat stay free of sexual or hateful content.
- **In the Discord:** questions before buying go in `#smp-faq`; suggestions, bugs, recruitment and trading
  go in `#smp-forum`; payment problems, reports and appeals go in a ticket. No spam or mass pings.
- **Enforcement:** a warning, then a temporary ban (1 to 7 days), then a permanent ban. Cheating, hate and
  doxxing can skip straight to a ban. Admins decide edge cases.
- **Appeals:** open a ticket (Front Desk) and say what happened, one appeal per decision. A different admin
  reviews it within 7 days.
- **Payments:** the Server Pass is the same price for everyone, and supporter tiers are cosmetic only. A ban
  doesn't refund time already paid for. If you can't get the modpack running in your first 48 hours, open a
  ticket for a refund. A chargeback on a pass is a permanent ban; open a ticket instead.

Enjoy!

- Rules 44 to 46 (2.8.0, Breaking Bedrock): the owners adopted all three on 27 October 2026. 44 and 45 stop
  griefing nobody can undo (a void hole under a base, a broken exit portal); 46 is the usual SMP ban, because
  the roof is a flat, empty, mob-free road over the whole Nether and the easiest escape route there is (compare

- Friends & Foes (2.8.0) adds the Totem of Freezing (from Iceologers) and the Totem of Illusion (from

What the server enforces by itself (two data packs from `tools/rules.py`, plus config defaults).
Run the commands from the server console or with `realms.ps1 rcon "<command>"`:

- **The short rules.** Players accept `codeofconduct/en_us.txt` (in the server folder) before they join,
  and again whenever it changes (`enable-code-of-conduct=true` in `server.properties`). It quotes the rule
  numbers in this file, so change it when a rule changes. A file edit takes effect at the next restart.
- **Rule 23, the launch hour.** At launch, run `function realms:rules/launch`. PvP and the Nether close
  (the `pvp` and `allow_entering_nether_using_portals` gamerules), and after 3600 seconds of server time
  both open again by themselves. The timer is saved with the world, so a restart doesn't reset it.
  The End is closed from the start: anyone who gets in is sent to the surface at spawn within a second.
  Open it with
  `function realms:rules/open_end`; `function realms:rules/close_end` closes it again.
- **Rule 24, no Netherite armor** (pack `realms-no-netherite-armor`). The smithing table won't upgrade
  diamond armor to Netherite. Netherite armor from mod loot (Incendium, Moog's structures) or from a mob is
  taken off once a second and dropped at the player's feet, so nothing is lost. Netherite tools, weapons
  and horse armor are untouched. To lift the rule: `datapack disable "file/realms-no-netherite-armor"`.
  The world remembers that across restarts and updates. `datapack enable` turns the rule back on.
- **Rule 25, the border.** The first time the server starts with the pack, the border is set to
  2500 x 2500, centred on spawn. Since Minecraft 26.3 every dimension has its own border, so the pack also
  gives the Nether the matching one at its 1:8 scale (312.5 wide, centred on spawn / 8). The End's is left
  alone. After that the borders are yours to grow, e.g. `worldborder set 4000 600` (600 seconds to widen)
  and `execute in minecraft:the_nether run worldborder set 500 600`. The pack never touches them again.
- **Rule 26, PvP grace** (pack `realms-rules`). The first time the server sees a player, they get grace
  (unless the launch hour is running) and, a few seconds later, the welcome screen. Someone who played here
  before the pack was installed (Minecraft's statistics show they've left the game before, or played over a
  minute) gets neither. Grace is an invisible
  item in the player's body slot whose enchantment blocks all damage a player causes; it comes back after
  a death and is off while the player is in the event arena. It ends after 48 hours of game time, which
  stops while the server is empty, when they hurt a player, or with `/trigger end_grace`.
  `function realms:rules/launch` ends everyone's. One player: `execute as <player> run function
  realms:rules/grace_end` (or `grace_start` to give one). Their grace ends at game time
  `scoreboard players get <player> realms.grace`; `time query gametime` is now.
- **Rule 26, the no-PvP spawn.** An Open Parties and Claims server claim over the 7 x 7 chunks around
  spawn (the vanilla spawn protection and a bit more). With their `playersByPlayers` exception off, server
  claims stop players hurting players, and they keep spawn's blocks safe too (rule 22). An admin sets it up
  once per world from the server console, whose commands run at the world spawn (in Docker:
  `realms rcon "<command>"`):
  `opac server-claims-config set claims.protection.exceptions.playersByPlayers false`, then
  `oclaims server claim in minecraft:overworld ~-48 ~-48 ~48 ~48`. The pack can't do it: Open Parties and
  Claims doesn't let a datapack function claim. Build the event arena outside this square, or nobody can
  fight in it. A chunk a player claimed there earlier stays theirs (the server claim skips it), so it isn't part
  of the no-PvP zone until they unclaim it.
- **Rule 29, 4-player factions.** Open Parties and Claims' `maxPartyMembers` is 4
  (`defaults/config/openpartiesandclaims-server.toml`). On a server installed before this setting
  shipped, the installer keeps your existing `config/openpartiesandclaims-server.toml`: set
  `maxPartyMembers = 4` there yourself (server stopped). Lowering it doesn't remove members already in a
  bigger party.
- **Rule 35, 2 x 2 chunks per member.** Open Parties and Claims' `maxPlayerClaims` is 4 in the same file,
  and claims belong to players, so a faction's claims add up to 2 x 2 chunks per member. On an older
  server, set it there yourself.
- **B9, Fire Aspect and Flame** (pack `realms-rules`). They're out of the enchanting table, loot, villager
  trades and mob gear. A copy that still turns up (a book in a mod's chest, a mob's weapon) shows as
  "(disabled)", does nothing, and can't be put on an item at an anvil. This replaces vanilla's
  `#minecraft:non_treasure` enchantment tag; if a future mod adds its enchantments to that tag, add them to
  `VANILLA_NON_TREASURE` in `tools/rules.py` too.
- **B15, B17 and B19, x-ray** (AntiXray, `defaults/config/antixray.toml`). The server never sends the real
  block for an ore that touches no air, water or lava. In the Overworld every such spot shows a random ore, so
  x-ray sees noise; in the Nether and the End hidden ores show as netherrack or end stone. X-ray clients and
  packs (B15), the cushion glitch (B17), F3+A and reloading shaders (B19) can only show what the server sent.
  Distant Horizons' own server-side LODs would carry the real ores, so the server doesn't send them ("Distant
  Horizons and x-ray" in `docs/ADMIN.md`). For x-ray that gets past all this (a cracked seed, say), MinerTrack alerts
  moderators when someone mines from one buried diamond vein straight to the next; it never punishes by itself.
  The rest of B15 (hacked clients, duping, CIT packs, health indicators) is still on admins, with BanHammer,
  Vanish, InvView and Ledger ("Moderation" in `docs/ADMIN.md`).
- **B18, Nova Structures' books** (Enchantment Disabler, `defaults/config/enchantmentdisabler/config.toml`,
  since 2.8.0). All 17 of Dungeons and Taverns' own enchantments (`nova_structures:*`) are out of the
  enchanting table, loot and villager trades, so nobody finds them; staff make event rewards with `/give` or
  `enchant`, which still work. On a server installed before 2.8.0 the file is new, so the installer copies
  it. If Dungeons and Taverns adds an enchantment, add it to the file (and to `tests/test_mod_defaults.py`).
- **Rule 43, shops** (Universal Shops). Shelved on 26 October 2026: the mod isn't in the pack, so the server
  enforces nothing for rule 43 now, and scams are on admins. The claims entry for shops stays in
  `defaults/config/openpartiesandclaims-server.toml`, dormant until they return. With shops back, anyone may
  click a Trade Shop to buy from it, even in someone else's claim: `forcedBlockProtectionExceptionList` lets an
  empty hand use `universal_shops:trade_block` there. The chest behind it stays protected, and nobody can put
  a shop on someone else's chest in a claim: placing one means clicking the chest, which the claim refuses.
  On a server installed before shops shipped, run `deploy/shops-claims.sh` (live VPS) or add the entry
  yourself, server stopped ("Shops" in `docs/ADMIN.md`).
- **Rules 44 to 46, bedrock** (Breaking Bedrock, no config). Nothing stops a player with a netherite pickaxe
  outside claims and spawn protection, so these are on admins, with Ledger's block log ("Mod settings" in
  `docs/ADMIN.md` has what was tested).
- **The welcome and the links** (pack `realms-rules`). A first join gets a welcome screen whose one button
  opens the Discord, where `#smp-forum` has the factions that are recruiting. Every player's pause menu
  has a Monkey SMP button with the Discord, the live map, the rules and the store. The addresses are in
  `tools/rules.py` (`DISCORD`, `LINKS`). Show someone the welcome again:
  `dialog show <player> realms:welcome`.

Still on players and admins: everything else, including rule 33 (leaving a faction needs an admin's
approval; the mod lets players leave a party freely).
