> **Survive. Trade. Steal hearts.**
>
> Monkey SMP is a free survival economy server with lifesteal PvP and short seasons. Java and Bedrock players share
> one world, and you don't need a single mod.
>
> **Get rich.** Everyone starts Season 1 with nothing, on the same day. Mine, farm and grind, sell at the server
> shop, and undercut your rivals in the auction house.
>
> **Steal hearts.** Spawn and your claimed base are safe. Everywhere else, a kill steals a heart. Run out and you
> spectate until the mid-season revival. Extra hearts are crafted or bought with in-game money, never real money.
>
> **Fair start.** New players get 48 hours of PvP grace to find their feet.
>
> **Seasons with an ending.** 8 to 10 weeks, weekly events built for clips, then the border shrinks, a boss rises and
> the season ends in a finale. Titles and cosmetics carry over. Everything else resets, so nobody is ever too far
> ahead to catch.
>
> **Free, for real.** No pass, no whitelist. The store sells cosmetics only: nothing that helps you fight, earn or
> survive, and no in-game money.
