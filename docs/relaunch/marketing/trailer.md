# Season 1 trailer brief

Draft for Lorenzo. A 45-second trailer (cut-downs at 30 s and 15 s), for YouTube, the site, and TikTok and Shorts
as 9:16 crops. The old trailer (`marketing/trailer.py`) is built from modded footage, so this needs new footage from
the closed beta (late January).

## The job

In 45 seconds a viewer should know: it's **free**, it's an **economy SMP with lifesteal**, **Java and Bedrock**, and
**Season 1 starts 6 February**. Every shot shows one of those, or a reason to care about one.

## Tone and look

Fast, playful, a little sly. Bright daylight footage, gold and heart red in the text cards (brand.md). Text on
screen does the talking; no voice-over needed. Music: an upbeat track with a hard drop at the first heart steal.
`marketing/music.py` made the last one; anything else needs a licence you hold.

## Filming notes

- Film on the beta server with real players where you can; where it's staged, say so in the description, as the
  old trailer's notes did.
- Bedrock footage needs a phone or tablet capture; it's the shot that sells crossplay, so get a clean one.
- Hide coordinates, real player names in chat (unless they agree), and the F3 screen.
- No Mojang logo or the word "Minecraft" in a way that suggests it's official, and nothing cape-like on the end card.
- Export 1920 x 1080 at 30 fps for YouTube and the site, and a 1080 x 1920 vertical crop for TikTok and Shorts with
  the text cards moved into the safe area away from the app buttons.
