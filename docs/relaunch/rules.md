# Monkey SMP rules (Season 1, relaunch)

The player rulebook for the relaunched server: free to join on Java and Bedrock, no mods needed. It replaces
`docs/RULES.md` (the modded season's rules) when the relaunch ships. Rule numbers are stable, so "rule 7"
means the same thing in game, on the site and in a ticket. How hearts, claims and safe zones work is in
`lifesteal.md`; this page says what is and isn't allowed.

## The short version

- Play fair: no cheats, x-ray, dupes or exploits.
- Claimed land can't be griefed or looted. Unclaimed land can. PvP is on everywhere except spawn and the market.
- One account per person. No kill trading.
- Be decent in chat. Keep it all-ages.
- Never buy or sell anything from the server for real money.

## Play fair

1. **No cheats.** No hacked clients, reach, kill aura, fly, speed, auto-clickers, macros, scaffold, or
   anything that plays for you. This covers Java and Bedrock alike (Toolbox, Horion, Zephyr and the like).
2. **No x-ray of any kind.** That includes x-ray texture packs, cave-finding minimaps, seed crackers, ore
   finders, freecam, and see-through-block glitches.
3. **Allowed client mods:** performance and visual mods (Sodium, Lithium, Iris and shaders), HUD and
   inventory tweaks, zoom, and minimaps *without* entity radar or cave view. Anything else, ask staff first.
4. **No dupes, exploits or glitches** that give you items, hearts or an edge. Found one? Report it in a
   ticket. Reporting one is always safe; using it gets you banned.
5. **No lag machines** or builds made to lag the server.

## Bases, claims and raiding

6. **Claimed land is protected.** Don't try to get into, break, or take from a claim you aren't trusted on,
   by any trick (pistons, lava, mobs, exploits). PvP still works inside claims: you can fight there, you
   just can't take the base apart.
7. **Unclaimed land is fair game.** Builds and chests outside a claim can be raided, and staff won't
   restore them. Claim what you care about: right-click two corners with a golden shovel.
8. **No claim blocking.** Don't claim land just to box in someone else's base, block a road, or wall off
   a structure from everyone.
9. **No traps or griefing at spawn or the market.** They are safe zones.
10. **No griefing the commons:** Nether highways, public farms and event areas staff mark as public.
11. **No holes in the bottom bedrock layer** and no traps that drop players or items into the void.

## PvP and hearts

12. **PvP is part of the game.** Killing someone outside a safe zone isn't against the rules, even if
    it's annoying. Repeatedly camping one player's spawn or bed is (rule 16).
13. **No combat logging.** Leaving while tagged kills you and costs at least one heart.
14. **No kill trading or heart farming.** Don't let friends, alts or anyone else kill you on purpose so
    hearts change hands, and don't farm the same player. Staff remove hearts gained this way.
15. **No escaping combat with glitches**, like logging into a block or exploiting the safe-zone edge.
16. **No harassment in game.** Hunting one player again and again after they've asked you to stop,
    spawn camping, or following them to grief is harassment, not PvP.

## Accounts and alts

17. **One account per person.** A second account, or playing on someone else's, needs staff approval.
    Alts used to dodge elimination, store hearts or carry heart items are banned with the main account.
18. **Eliminated means out.** Don't come back on another account while you wait to be revived.
19. **You are responsible for your account.** "My brother did it" doesn't undo a ban.

## Chat and community

20. **Respect people.** No slurs, hate speech, threats, sexual content, or harassment, in game chat, signs,
    books, names, skins or builds. Trash talk about the game is fine; going after the person is not.
21. **Keep it all-ages.** Everything on the server is visible to kids.
22. **No personal information.** Don't share anyone's real name, address, photos or accounts, even as a joke.
23. **No spam or advertising.** No repeated messages, caps floods, other servers, or links that sell things.
24. **No impersonating staff**, and don't threaten to report people to scare them.

## Bedrock and Java together

25. **Java and Bedrock play by the same rules.** Being on a phone or console isn't an excuse to use a
    modded client, and being on Java isn't an excuse to target Bedrock players' controls with exploits.
26. Things that differ between the two editions (some redstone, bridging, hit cooldowns) are not
    exploits. Bugs that only work on one edition and break the game are (rule 4).

## Money

27. **No real-money trading.** Don't buy or sell items, hearts, land, ranks, in-game money or accounts for
    real money, gift cards, crypto or items in another game. The official store is the only place real money goes.
28. **In-game deals are at your own risk.** A broken in-game trade is part of the game; a scam involving
    real money, accounts or personal information is not (rules 22 and 27).

## What happens when you break a rule

Staff pick the step that fits. Severe rules skip straight to a ban. Anything gained by breaking a rule
(items, hearts, land) is taken back, and griefing in claims is rolled back.

| Kind | Rules | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | After that |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minor | 8, 9, 10, 23, 24 | Warning | 1-hour mute or kick | 24-hour mute or 1-day ban | 7-day ban |
| Serious | 5, 6, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21 | 1-day ban | 7-day ban | 30-day ban | Permanent |
| Severe | 1, 2, 4, 18, 22, 27 | 30-day ban | Permanent | | |

- A punishment covers every account of the person. Dodging a ban with an alt resets the ladder to a
  permanent ban.
- Doxxing, real threats, and sexual content involving minors are permanent with no appeal, and may be
  reported to the platform or the police.
- A ban doesn't take your hearts, but anything gained unfairly is removed.
- Warnings expire after 60 days; bans stay on your record for the season.

## Appeals

- Open a ticket in the Discord, or use the contact form on the website if you're not on the Discord.
- Say which punishment you're appealing and why. One appeal per decision.
- A staff member who didn't give the punishment reviews it within 7 days.
- If the appeal is upheld, the punishment is lifted and anything removed is given back where it can be.
- Lying in an appeal, or appealing from an alt, loses the appeal.
- Permanent bans for rules 1, 2 and 4 can be appealed once, 30 days after the ban.
