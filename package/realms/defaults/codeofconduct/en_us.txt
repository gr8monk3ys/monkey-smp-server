Monkey SMP: the short rules

The full rulebook is in #smp-rules on the Discord. Rule numbers match it.

Fights
- No combat logging: don't log out in a fight, or 3 minutes into a chase. (3)
- No farming fresh-respawned players, no AFK killing, no killing players who are lagging out. (5, 13, 14)
- No lava PvP, no elytra in fights, no Happy Ghast PvP. (16-18)
- No pearls, totems, enchanted golden apples, crystals, bed bombs, Fire Aspect or Flame. (B3-B5, B7-B9)

Factions and land
- A faction holds at most 4 players, and you stay in yours for the season. (29, 33)
- Your territory is 2 x 2 chunks per member, walled or inside a base, marked with your banner. (35, 36)
- Nobody griefs a territory without permission. Admins roll that back; legal raids stay. (37, 42)
- No insiding: never grief, raid or rob your own faction, or use what you had as a member after you
  leave. (40)
- No teaming: stay out of fights between other factions, except at events. (41)

New players and spawn
- Your first 48 hours come with PvP grace. Attacking a player ends it. (26)
- Spawn is a no-PvP zone, and nobody touches it. (22, 26)

Everyone
- No hacked clients, x-ray, dupes or exploits. (B13-B17, B19)
- No holes into the void, no breaking End portals or gateways, nothing on the Nether roof. (44-46)
- Respect people: no slurs, harassment, threats or doxxing, anywhere.
- No real-money trading, and no account sharing. (21)

Breaking a rule means a warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban. Cheating, hate and
doxxing can go straight to a ban. Appeals go in a Discord ticket.
