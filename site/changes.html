What changed

Every change to the rules, the mods and the wardrobe that you'd notice in game, newest first. The same notes go in the Discord's #changelog channel the day they go live.

Updates

Next updateGrace for new players, the short rules, and more wardrobe skins

  • You accept the short rules before you join, and again whenever they change.
  • Rule 26: a new player gets 48 hours of PvP grace, and other players can't hurt them. Hitting a player or /trigger end_grace ends it early, and it's off in the event arena. If you played here before this update, you don't get it.
  • Rule 26: spawn is a no-PvP zone (the 7 by 7 chunks around it).
  • New rules 40 to 42: no insiding, no teaming (helping someone outside your faction in a fight; events are exempt), and rollbacks (griefing a territory is rolled back, a legal raid isn't).
  • Rule 35: each player claims at most 4 chunks, so a faction's land is 2 by 2 chunks per member.
  • Villagers boxed into a 1 by 1 space update less often, to save the server work, and animals stop breeding at 32 of a kind within 64 blocks.
  • A welcome screen on your first join, and a Monkey SMP button in the pause menu with the Discord, the live map, the rules and the store.
  • Eight new skins. The Oathblades I, II and III unlock at 1, 2 and 3 months of pass. Rallier unlocks at 3 events attended and the Plumed Helm at 10.
  • Holiday skins, earned only by being at that holiday's event: Gravelight at Hallowtide (late October), the Midwinter Cap at Midwinter (late December) and Solstice at Midsummer (late June). They are never sold. If you miss one, it comes back at next year's event.
  • The wardrobe (/trigger wardrobe) now has two pages: skins earned in game, and pass and supporter skins.
  • You get an "Unlocked" message in chat when you earn a skin.
  • Your skins, days and hours played, supporter color and titles carry over into the next season's world.
  • Update the modpack when this goes live. It has the new skins' textures.

25 October 2026The live map

  • The live map has a new address: map.monkeysmp.lscaturchio.xyz.
  • It no longer shows where players are.
  • It stops at the world border, and the Nether map shows the caves instead of the bedrock roof.
  • Nothing to install.

25 October 2026Monkey SMP and the Season I rules

  • The server is now called Monkey SMP (it was The Realms).
  • The server enforces the Season I rules it can. The world border is 2500 by 2500 around spawn, and the Nether's is 1:8. There's no PvP and no Nether in the first hour after launch, and the End stays closed until Dragon Night. Netherite armor is off until Netherite age, and Fire Aspect and Flame are disabled. A faction holds at most four players.
  • The wardrobe: /trigger wardrobe puts a skin on your sword or helmet. It changes the look only, never damage, armor or durability. Skins unlock with days played, months of pass, friends invited, supporter tiers, and for playing in Season I.
  • Arena Glory: /trigger arena set 1 to join when staff open it. You get a kit, everyone's Strength is +3 inside, and nothing goes in or out with you.
  • Update the modpack. It adds the wardrobe's resource pack.

11 October 2026New dungeons and structures

  • New: Epic Dungeons, Moog's Voyager Structures, Grand Capitals, Better Underwater Ruins, Slabs & Stairs, Copper Rails, More Villagers, More Mobs, Stormie's Spiders, Sulfur Caves Plus and about 20 smaller mods.
  • Gone: Dungeons and Taverns (its structure overhauls stay) and Epic Structures: Villages.
  • A full server runs smoother: mobs far from players update less often.
  • Reinstall the modpack (about 520 MB).

6 October 2026An easier install, and a mod sync

  • A one-click installer for the normal Minecraft Launcher, so you don't need the Modrinth App or Prism.
  • New: Craftable Cobwebs, Players Drop Heads, Visible Traders. Gone: Dragonkind Evolved, the Dungeons and Taverns mineshaft overhaul, Just Player Heads.
  • Room for 30 players (was 20).

5 October 2026Fabric 26.3 and a new world

  • The server moved to Fabric on Minecraft 26.3, so the whole mod list runs, on a new world.
  • Factions are parties with allies and land claims (Open Parties and Claims).
  • Spawn is protected within 48 blocks, and staff can roll back griefing.
