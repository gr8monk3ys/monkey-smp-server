The server Pricing Terms Discord

Make videos on Monkey SMP.

If you stream or post videos from the server, you can have a creator code. Viewers who enter it when they buy a pass pay the same as everyone else, and you get a share of what they paid. Small channels are welcome: the server holds 30 players, so we'd rather have a few creators who play here than a big one who visits once.

The terms

  1. What you earn

    20% of every 3-month pass bought with your code: $2.80 of the $14. Tebex, our store, pays it into your Tebex Wallet and holds each payment for 30 working days first, in case of refunds and chargebacks.

  2. Codes are for the 3-month pass

    Tebex pays a code's share only on the first payment of a subscription, and the first payment of a monthly pass is $5. So send people to the 3-month pass, where your share is worth having.

  3. No discount for buyers

    Mojang's rules for paid servers say access costs the same for everyone, so a code takes nothing off the price. It only tells us who sent the buyer.

  4. Say that you're paid

    Whenever you show or mention your code, say that you earn from it: #ad in the title or post, or "I get a share of passes bought with my code" said out loud, and the platform's own paid-promotion label where there is one. The FTC requires it in the US, and Mojang's rules for servers ask for it too.

  5. Describe the server as it is

    A pass buys access, supporter tiers buy looks, and nothing buys an advantage. Don't promise items, ranks, slots or anything else the server doesn't sell.

  6. The rules still apply

    You play under the same rules as everyone else, recording or not. Many players are under 18: if someone asks to stay off your stream or out of your video, keep them out.

  7. Either side can stop

    You can drop your code, and we can switch it off, at any time. A code is switched off at once for breaking these terms. Shares already earned are paid out as Tebex pays them.

Tebex pays creators under its own terms, which may ask you for details before it pays out.

Getting a code

Open a ticket in the Discord with a link to your channel and the code you'd like (your channel name works). We'll make it in the store and tell you when it works. To be paid, set up a Tebex Wallet when Tebex asks you to.

The store isn't open yet, so no code pays out until it opens. Until then, passes are arranged in the Discord.
