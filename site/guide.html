How the server works

What happens when you join, how factions and land work, what a week brings, and every command you can type, by rank. New here? Read the first two parts. The rest is for when you need it.

Your first hour

Get a pass and the modpack first (how to join). Then:

  • Talk to the Guide at spawn: right-click it with an empty hand. It has the short rules, how to find a faction, and buttons for this week's challenges, the faction standings and your wardrobe. The Town Crier next to it has this week's Saturday event and next week's.
  • You're safe for 48 hours after your first join: other players can't hurt you. Attacking someone ends it early, and so does /trigger end_grace. Grace is for settling in, not for scouting or carrying loot for a faction.
  • Spawn is safe. Nobody can fight in the 7 × 7 chunks around it, and nobody but admins can build within 48 blocks of it. PvP is on everywhere else.
  • Dying drops your things where you died, as in plain Minecraft.
  • Voice chat works by distance: press V for its settings. M opens the world map, and the live map shows the world in your browser (not where players are).

On launch night

Season I opens on Saturday 7 November 2026 at 5 PM Pacific (8 PM Eastern). For the first hour PvP, the Nether and the End are closed; then PvP and the Nether open. The End opens on the night the Town Crier announces. Netherite armor stays off until staff say so (Netherite tools and weapons are fine). Be online for 10 minutes on launch weekend and you keep the Founder's Circlet skin.

Factions and land

A faction is a party of up to four players under its own banner. Once you join one you stay in it for the season; leaving needs an admin's approval.

  • Claim land on the world map: press M and right-click chunks, or stand in a chunk and type /oclaims claim. Outsiders can't break, build or open anything in your claim. The claims screen shows how many chunks you have left.
  • Allies share claims. Alliances are fine; a group of more than four that always plays together is a confederation and breaks rule 32.
  • War and pacts. Your faction's leader can declare war on another faction: until Sunday at 7 PM Pacific, kills between the two count twice in the weekly standings. Or offer a pact: no war between you until one side breaks it (two pacts at most). Neither changes what claims protect or what the rules allow.
  • Trading is by hand, items for items: there's no money, shop or auction house. Lying about a trade is a scam and breaks the rules.

A week on the server

The same rhythm every week of the season. Times are Pacific.

  1. Monday

    12 AMThree new challenges: one solo, one for factions, one exploring.

    6–10 PMA supply drop, once 4 or more are on.

  2. Tuesday

    6–10 PMA supply drop, once 4 or more are on.

  3. Wednesday

    6–10 PMA supply drop, once 4 or more are on.

  4. Thursday

    6–10 PMA supply drop, once 4 or more are on.

  5. Friday

    No drop. Build, trade, get ready for Saturday.

  6. Omens

    One or two weekday evenings, with no warning: a Blood Moon, a Meteor, a Truce Hour or a Hunted player.

  7. Saturday

    6 PMThe week's event: a Faction Raid, Arena Glory, a Build Battle, or the border breaking open.

  8. Sunday

    7 PMThe week's faction standings close and go up in the Discord. Wars end.

The Town Crier at spawn has the next two Saturday events.

Supply drops

Chat gives the coordinates and a boss bar counts down 10 minutes. Then chests of food, iron, arrows and the like land out in the newest land, away from every claim, and vanish 20 minutes later. The usual rules apply out there.

Omens

  • Blood Moon: a night on Hard, with tougher mobs that drop a little extra.
  • Meteor: a richer supply drop, with 5 minutes' warning.
  • Truce Hour: no PvP anywhere for an hour.
  • Hunted: the player on the longest kill streak gets a bounty for 30 minutes.

Standings and outposts

Every faction gets a rank each week from four things: event wins (the Cup), land claimed, its members' Strength and its kills. Bounties and outposts add points on top.

Four outposts stand in the newest land, one each to the north, east, south and west. Stand in one with your faction, and nobody else's, for 60 seconds to take it. Each hour you hold it while another faction is online is a point, up to 20 a week. /trigger outposts shows where they are and who holds them.

Firsts and skins

The first player to reach each of 14 milestones (the first diamond, the first into the Nether, the first to slay the dragon and so on) is announced to everyone and keeps it all season: /trigger firsts. Skins for swords and helmets unlock as you play, come to events, finish challenges and stay on. They only change how an item looks, never what it does: /trigger wardrobe.

The season

A season lasts about 12 weeks. The world border starts at 2500 × 2500 and breaks open twice; the End opens on Dragon Night; a finale ends it, and then the world locks.

Commands by rank

Each rank can do everything the ranks above it can. Supporters and faction leaders are players with a few extras, not staff.

Player
Play, claim land, join a faction, use voice chat, open the menus below.
Faction leader
The owner of a faction's party: invites members and allies, declares war and makes pacts.
Supporter
Ember, Banner or Crown: a name color, a title and skins. No commands of their own.
Builder
A player staff trust with build jobs. An admin switches them to creative for a job and back after.
Moderator
Warns, mutes, kicks and bans; watches quietly; checks and undoes griefing.
Admin
Everything: ranks, the whitelist, events, and the server's own commands.

Players

Menus open in chat; click a line to use it. Type the start of a command and press Tab to see the rest.

Menus

/trigger wardrobe
Your skins: the ones you've unlocked, and how to unlock the rest.
/trigger challenges
This week's three challenges and how far you've got. Hover one for what counts.
/trigger standings
This week's faction standings so far.
/trigger diplomacy
How your faction stands with every other: wars, pacts, offers.
/trigger outposts
The four outposts: where, who holds them, and your faction's hours.
/trigger firsts
The season's Server Firsts and who holds each.
/trigger end_grace
Ends your 48 hours of safety now. There's no getting it back.

Factions and land

/oparties create
Starts a party: your faction. You're its leader.
/oparties join <leader>
Joins the party that invited you, named by its leader.
/oclaims claim
Claims the chunk you're standing in. Or press M and right-click chunks.
/opac
Your party and claims settings. /oclaims and /oparties with Tab list everything else.

Events

/trigger arena set 1
Joins Arena Glory while it's open: stand by the entrance, in survival, with an empty inventory, armor and offhand.
/trigger arena set 2
Leaves the arena. You come out with what you went in with.

Keys

V
Voice chat settings.
M
The world map, where you claim land.
Sneak + right-click
With both hands empty: picks up a passive animal or a block like an enchanting table. Right-click to put it down. Nothing that stores items, and nothing in someone else's claim.

Faction leaders

The player who made the party leads it. Everything players can do, plus:

/oparties member invite <player>
Invites a player. Four is the most a faction can have.
/oparties ally add <leader>
Makes another party an ally. Allies share claims.
/trigger diplomacy
For a leader it has buttons: declare war, offer a pact, accept one, break one. One move every 2 minutes. Wars and offers end at Sunday's close; pacts stand until broken.

Supporters

Nothing to type. Your name color shows by itself, and your skins appear in /trigger wardrobe with an "Unlocked" line in chat. Banner and Crown supporters tell staff the title they want, and staff set it.

Moderators

Typed in game, without the / where shown. Every punishment needs a reason, which the player sees and the next moderator can look up. Start the reason with -s to keep it out of public chat. Durations look like 30m, 12h, 3d or 1d12h.

Punishments, in the rules' order

/warn NAME reason
Step 1. Five warnings ban a player for 5 hours by themselves.
/tempban NAME 3d reason
Step 2: a ban of 1 to 7 days.
/ban NAME reason
Step 3, or straight away for cheating, hate or doxxing.
/tempmute NAME 1h reason
No chat or private messages for a while. /mute is for good. Voice chat isn't covered: ask an admin.
/kick NAME reason
Out of the server, free to come back.
/unban NAME
Also /unmute and /unwarn.

Watching quietly

/vanish
Hides you from players, the tab list and the server list until you run it again.
/view inv NAME
Looks into a player's inventory; /view echest NAME their ender chest. Look only.
/mtrack check NAME
A player's mining alerts. Lucky players set it off too, so look before you act. /mtrack reset NAME clears them.

Griefing

/ledger inspect
Turns inspect on or off; then click a block to see who changed it.
/ledger search source:NAME range:30 after:2h
What NAME did in the last 2 hours within 30 blocks.
/ledger rollback source:NAME range:30 after:2h
Undoes it. /ledger restore with the same words redoes it.
/ledger tp minecraft:overworld X Y Z
Takes you to a spot (the Nether is minecraft:the_nether). Or click "Click to teleport" in Ledger's results.

Moderators can't teleport to a player, move someone else, change game modes or whitelist anyone: those need an admin.

Admins

Every mod's commands, plus Minecraft's own (/tp, /gamemode, /give, /whitelist and the rest), which need op as well as the rank.

People

/lp user NAME parent set moderator
Makes NAME a moderator. parent remove moderator takes it back.
/lp user NAME permission settemp voicechat.speak false 1d
Mutes NAME's voice for a day.
/ban-ip
Admins only: an address is often a whole household.
/gamemode creative NAME
Puts a builder in creative for a job; /gamemode survival NAME when it's done.
/view inv NAME
For an admin, items can be taken out too.

The season's systems

Each one lists its own commands in chat, clickable.

/function realms:events/help
Events: the arena, Anarchy, the Faction Raid, flags, books and the Cup.
/function realms:standings/help
The standings board and bounties.
/function realms:diplomacy/help
Faction numbers, and ending a war or pact by hand.
/function realms:drops/help
Supply drops: status, a drop now, on and off.
/function realms:omens/help
Omens: status, start, stop, on and off.
/function realms:outposts/help
Outposts: status, freeing one, on and off.

Building

//wand
WorldEdit's selection wand; //pos1 and //pos2 set the corners, //undo takes a change back.
/lp user NAME permission set worldedit.* true
Lends WorldEdit to a builder; permission unset worldedit.* takes it back.
