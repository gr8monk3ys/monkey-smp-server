How to join

  1. Get a pass

    Buy it with your Minecraft Java name. You're on the whitelist within a minute or two, and you get the member role in the Discord.

  2. Install the modpack

    The server adds blocks, mobs and items, so you need the pack (about 520 MB). Import it into the Modrinth App or Prism Launcher, or use the installer for the regular Minecraft Launcher. Get the modpack.

  3. Connect

    Multiplayer, Add Server, therealmsmc.lscaturchio.xyz. No port needed. The game wants about 6 GB of memory and a mid-range graphics card.

Rules

Short version: fight hard, play fair, don't be cruel. Staff decide edge cases; you can appeal any decision in the Discord.

  1. Respect people

    No slurs, hate, harassment, threats or sharing anyone's personal information, in game, in voice or in the Discord. Trash talk about the game is fine; going after the person is not.

  2. No cheating

    No hacked clients, x-ray (mods, resource packs or glitches), combat macros, auto-clickers or entity radar. Play with the modpack; shaders and client-side performance mods are fine. If you're not sure about a mod, ask first.

  3. Report exploits, don't use them

    No duplication glitches, lag machines or chunk-loading tricks. Report bugs privately in the Discord; a real find earns a thank-you title.

  4. Claims are the line

    Inside a claim, the owner's settings decide. Outside claims everything is contested: PvP, looting and taking down unclaimed builds are part of the game. Claim what you want to keep.

  5. Fight fair

    No PvP at spawn. Don't log out to escape a fight. Don't camp someone's respawn: after you kill a player, leave them alone for five minutes.

  6. Strength is earned

    Strength comes from real fights. No feeding: killing a friend, an ally or an alt over and over to move strength around, or trading kills for items.

  7. One account each

    No alternate accounts to dodge a ban, stretch claim limits or feed your own strength.

  8. No real-money trading

    Don't sell or buy items, land, strength or accounts for real money or gift cards.

  9. Keep the server fast

    Farms are welcome; giant ones that drag everyone's tick rate down are not. If a build causes lag, staff will talk to you before changing anything.

  10. Keep it all-ages

    Names, skins, signs, builds and chat stay free of sexual or hateful content.

The full rulebook is pinned in the Discord. Enforcement goes warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. Cheating, hate and doxxing can skip straight to a ban. A ban doesn't refund time already paid for.

Questions

Why does it cost money?

A modded server this size needs a fast dedicated CPU to stay smooth, and the pass keeps out players who are only there to wreck things. The pass pays for the machine; anything above that goes back into it.

Can I play on Bedrock, console or mobile?

No. The Realms is Java Edition with mods, so it needs a computer: Windows, macOS or Linux.

How many players?

We cap the server where it stays at full speed, about 25 online at once. When it fills up, we open a waitlist before we add anyone.

Refunds?

If you can't get the pack to run in your first 48 hours, ask in the Discord and you get your money back. After that, passes don't renew unless you keep them on, so you can stop any time.

I'm under 18.

Welcome. Ask a parent or guardian before buying anything. The server and the Discord are moderated all-ages spaces.

What happens when the server updates?

Updates are announced in the Discord, with a five-minute warning in chat. When the mod list changes, the Modrinth App updates the pack for you; otherwise reinstall it from the modpack link.
