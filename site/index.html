How to join

  1. Get a pass

    Buy it with your Minecraft Java name. You're on the whitelist within a minute or two, and you get the member role in the Discord.

  2. Install the modpack

    The server adds blocks, mobs and items, so you need the pack (about 520 MB). Import it into the Modrinth App or Prism Launcher, or use the installer for the regular Minecraft Launcher. Get the modpack.

  3. Connect

    Multiplayer, Add Server, monkeysmp.lscaturchio.xyz. No port needed. The game wants about 6 GB of memory and a mid-range graphics card.

Rules

Season I, hosted by Diego and Lorenzo Scaturchio. The full rulebook, every numbered rule and ban, is pinned in the Discord; if a rule turns out unfun or unfair, it changes with player feedback.

  1. Season I launch

    For the first hour, PvP, the Nether and the End are closed; then PvP and only the Nether open. No Netherite armor until we say so (Netherite tools and weapons are fine). The world border is 2500 × 2500 and grows as the server does.

  2. Factions and territory

    Factions of up to four, public, each with a banner. Alliances yes, confederations no. Your territory is 2 × 2 chunks a player, marked with your banner, or the inside of your base or walls at least 3 blocks tall. Nobody griefs, robs or farms a territory; everything outside one is fair game. Every base needs one feature visible from the surface.

  3. Fight fair

    You're in combat once someone hits you. No combat logging, no killing naked or AFK players, no restocking mid-fight, and no rejoining a team fight for 10 minutes after you die. Land elytra 50 blocks out, no elytra or lava in combat, no Happy Ghast PvP, and stay within about 30 blocks of shore.

  4. Strength

    Craft Strength only up to +3. Use Strength you're given right away unless you're at +5, and don't ender-chest it below +5. No Strength on arrows or on other players.

  5. Banned

    Enchanted maces, ender pearls and enchanted golden apples (crafting only), totems, crystal PvP, bed bombing, Fire Aspect and Flame, TNT minecarts, escape tunnels, and debuffs on others, except Slowness I, Weakness I, Poison I, Instant Harming I and Turtle Master from a bow or crossbow.

  6. No cheating

    No hacked clients, x-ray of any kind (including the cushion glitch, F3+A and reloading shaders), duping, health indicators, CIT packs, or Replay Mod and Flashback for an edge. No exploits, no loopholes, no account sharing. Not sure? Ask an admin first.

  7. Spawn is sacred

    Nobody tampers with spawn or the admin building, and no TNT traps there.

  8. Respect people

    No slurs, hate, harassment or sharing anyone's personal information. Keep it all-ages, no stream sniping without permission, and no buying or selling anything for real money.

Enforcement goes warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. Cheating, hate and doxxing can skip straight to a ban. A ban doesn't refund time already paid for.

Questions

Why does it cost money?

A modded server this size needs a fast dedicated CPU to stay smooth, and the pass keeps out players who are only there to wreck things. The pass pays for the machine; anything above that goes back into it.

Can I play on Bedrock, console or mobile?

No. Monkey SMP is Java Edition with mods, so it needs a computer: Windows, macOS or Linux.

How many players?

We cap the server where it stays at full speed, about 25 online at once. When it fills up, we open a waitlist before we add anyone.

Refunds?

If you can't get the pack to run in your first 48 hours, ask in the Discord and you get your money back. After that, passes don't renew unless you keep them on, so you can stop any time.

I'm under 18.

Welcome. Ask a parent or guardian before buying anything. The server and the Discord are moderated all-ages spaces.

What happens when the server updates?

Updates are announced in the Discord, with a five-minute warning in chat. When the mod list changes, the Modrinth App updates the pack for you; otherwise reinstall it from the modpack link.
