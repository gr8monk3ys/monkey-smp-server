<Entry date="2026-10-29" title="Monkey SMP, all the way through">

- The last traces of the server's old name are gone, including inside the modpack: the wardrobe's skins now load
  from Monkey SMP's own resource pack folder.
- With the Minecraft Launcher installer, your game folder moves to `.monkey-smp` by itself and keeps your worlds,
  screenshots and settings. The launcher shows a single "Monkey SMP" installation.
- **Update the modpack: it's a small download, and wardrobe skins don't show without it.**

</Entry>

<Entry date="2026-10-29" title="Season I moves to 7 November">

- Season I opens on Saturday 7 November at 5 PM Pacific (8 PM Eastern), a week later than planned. Week 1 starts
  on Monday 9 November and the finale is on 23 January. The Town Crier at spawn has the new dates for every event.
- The Guide and the Town Crier at spawn are opening up to everyone: once staff finish a settings step after this
  update, right-click them with an empty hand. Until then only staff can.
- Nothing to update: the modpack is the same.

</Entry>

<Entry date="2026-10-27" title="A new End, new dungeons, and carrying things around">

- Season I starts on a new world, so the new structures are everywhere: a remade End with its own villages, and
  new dungeons and taverns in the Overworld. Your wardrobe skins and supporter tier carry over; builds, items,
  claims and Strength from before launch don't.
- Carry On: sneak and right-click with both hands empty to pick up a passive animal, a beehive with its bees or a
  block like an enchanting table. Anything that stores items (chests, barrels, shulker boxes, furnaces, hoppers
  and the like), spawners, beds, doors, banners, players and the NPCs at spawn can't be carried, and nothing in
  someone else's territory.
- A structure's loot chest that you empty fills again a week later. Once opened, only staff can break it.
- A netherite pickaxe breaks bedrock, slowly. Three new rules come with it: no holes into the void, no breaking
  the End's exit portal or gateways, and nothing on top of the Nether roof.
- New mobs (Friends & Foes), mobs in random sizes, emotes, minecarts you can chain together, more recipes
  (craftable tridents, tipped arrows from any potion), decorative block variants with a chisel, and Channeling
  tridents that call lightning in any weather.
- The new dungeons' own enchantments are event rewards only, so you won't find them in chests.
- Dying drops your things on the ground again: corpses are gone. So are a few mods that didn't get on with the
  rest: Jade's block tooltips, Sound Physics, AmbientSounds, More Mobs, More Babies, Realistic Bees and Illager
  chatter.
- **Update the modpack (about 560 MB): you can't join without it.**

</Entry>

<Entry date="2026-10-26" title="A faster server, supply drops and a guide at spawn">

- The server is many times faster with players on. A mod added in an earlier update made every tick slow once
  people joined; it's gone.
- Supply drops, from Season I's first week. Monday to Thursday, 6 to 10 PM Pacific, once 4 or more players are on,
  chat and a boss bar give a drop's coordinates 10 minutes before it lands: one chest for every 4 players on, up
  to 3, outside every territory near the edge of the border. The chests disappear after 20 minutes. Inside: food,
  iron, coal, arrows, torches, logs and XP bottles.
- Two new faces at spawn. The Guide greets new players with the short rules, how to find a faction, and buttons
  for this week's challenges, the faction standings and the wardrobe. The Town Crier tells you this week's
  Saturday event and the next.
- **Update the modpack: the spawn NPCs need a new mod (Easy NPC) on your side too, and you can't join without
  it.**

</Entry>

<Entry date="2026-10-26" title="Season I's date, standings, challenges and a lighter pack">

- Season I opens on Saturday 21 November 2026 at 5 PM Pacific (8 PM Eastern), with everyone let in at once. The
  front page counts down to it in your own time zone.
- Every faction gets a rank each week, from four things: Season Cup points, claimed land, its members' Strength,
  and their PvP kills that week outside the event arena.
- The week closes on Sunday at 7 PM Pacific: the final table goes up in Discord and in chat.
- The board at spawn shows this week so far (updated every hour) and last week's top five, and `/trigger
  standings` shows it anywhere.
- Bounties: staff post them on the strongest players. Win one in a fair fight and you get the Bounty Hunter title
  and 5 standings points for your faction that week. Bounties pay no coins, and nothing about them is sold.
- New rule 43: trade by hand, items for items, since there's no money on the server. Lying about what you're
  trading is a scam, and scammers give back what they took.
- Slow computer? Shaders, Distant Horizons, the resource packs and most visual and sound mods are now optional.
  Untick them in Prism Launcher, or switch them off in the Modrinth App's Content tab.
- Three challenges every week: one solo, one for your faction and one for exploring, about one to three hours of
  play in all. A new set starts every Monday at 12 AM Pacific, after the Saturday event.
- `/trigger challenges` shows this week's three and how far you are. Hover over one to see what counts. The
  wardrobe has a third page with them too.
- Only this week's play counts. Blocks you place and break again don't count, and player kills never do.
- Three new skins for challenges done: Trailblazer at 3, the Vanguard Helm at 9 and Pinnacle at 18. Your count
  carries over into the next season.
- Founder's Circlet, a helm skin: play 10 minutes on Season I's launch weekend, from Saturday 21 November at 5 PM
  Pacific to the end of Sunday. It is never sold and never comes back.
- **Update the modpack: it has the new skins' textures and the tick boxes for the optional files.**

</Entry>

<Entry date="2026-10-26" title="Grace for new players, the short rules, and more wardrobe skins">

- X-ray mods and resource packs no longer show buried ores: the server only sends ores you could see.
- Distant Horizons draws far-off land from what you have explored yourself. The server no longer sends its own
  distant terrain.
- The server restarts every day at 4:30 AM Pacific, with warnings in chat 5 minutes and 1 minute before.
- You accept the short rules before you join, and again whenever they change.
- Rule 26: a new player gets 48 hours of PvP grace, and other players can't hurt them. Hitting a player or
  `/trigger end_grace` ends it early, and it's off in the event arena. If you played here before this update, you
  don't get it.
- Rule 26: spawn is a no-PvP zone (the 7 by 7 chunks around it).
- New rules 40 to 42: no insiding, no teaming (helping someone outside your faction in a fight; events are
  exempt), and rollbacks (griefing a territory is rolled back, a legal raid isn't).
- Rule 35: each player claims at most 4 chunks, so a faction's land is 2 by 2 chunks per member.
- Villagers boxed into a 1 by 1 space update less often, to save the server work, and animals stop breeding at 32
  of a kind within 64 blocks.
- A welcome screen on your first join, and a Monkey SMP button in the pause menu with the Discord, the live map,
  the rules and the store.
- Eight new skins. The Oathblades I, II and III unlock at 1, 2 and 3 months of pass. Rallier unlocks at 3 events
  attended and the Plumed Helm at 10.
- Holiday skins, earned only by being at that holiday's event: Gravelight at Hallowtide (late October), the
  Midwinter Cap at Midwinter (late December) and Solstice at Midsummer (late June). They are never sold. If you
  miss one, it comes back at next year's event.
- The wardrobe (`/trigger wardrobe`) now has two pages: skins earned in game, and pass and supporter skins.
- You get an "Unlocked" message in chat when you earn a skin.
- Your skins, days and hours played, supporter color and titles carry over into the next season's world.
- **Update the modpack. It has the new skins' textures.**

</Entry>

<Entry date="2026-10-25" title="Monkey SMP and the Season I rules">

- The server is now called Monkey SMP (it was Monkey SMP).
- The server enforces the Season I rules it can. The world border is 2500 by 2500 around spawn, and the Nether's
  is 1:8. There's no PvP and no Nether in the first hour after launch, and the End stays closed until Dragon
  Night. Netherite armor is off until Netherite age, and Fire Aspect and Flame are disabled. A faction holds at
  most four players.
- The wardrobe: `/trigger wardrobe` puts a skin on your sword or helmet. It changes the look only, never damage,
  armor or durability. Skins unlock with days played, months of pass, friends invited, supporter tiers, and for
  playing in Season I.
- Arena Glory: `/trigger arena set 1` to join when staff open it. You get a kit, everyone's Strength is +3 inside,
  and nothing goes in or out with you.
- **Update the modpack. It adds the wardrobe's resource pack.**

</Entry>
