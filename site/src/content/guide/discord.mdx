Everything Monkey SMP has on the <ConfigLink to="DISCORD_INVITE">Discord</ConfigLink> sits in one category,
**Monkey SMP**. Don't see it? Pick **Minecraft** under **Channels & Roles** at the top of the channel list.

## The channels

- **#smp-rules**: the rulebook, short. The full one is on [the front page](/#rules).
- **#smp-faq**: the pass, the modpack, joining, refunds.
- **#smp-commands**: the commands players type in game and on the Discord, the same as
  [this guide's](/guide/commands/players).
- **#monkey-smp**: chat with the game. What you say here shows up in game chat, and once you've linked your
  name, what you say in game shows up here. The server's status is pinned at the top.
- **#founding-roster**: sign up for launch night, one message each.
- **#smp-forum**: register a faction, find one, report a bug or suggest something.

Payment problems, reports and appeals go in a ticket in **#help**, not in these channels.

## Commands on the Discord

Type these in any channel. Only you see the answer, except for <code>/smp top</code>, which everyone in the
channel sees.

<dl className="cmds">
  <div><dt><code>/smp link &lt;minecraft name&gt;</code></dt><dd>Connects your Minecraft Java name to your Discord
    name, so your game chat shows up in #monkey-smp and your playtime counts. It doesn't give you access to the
    server: that's the pass.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>/smp unlink</code></dt><dd>Forgets your linked name. Your pass and access stay.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>/smp status</code></dt><dd>Is the server up, who's on, and your own link and playtime.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>/smp top</code></dt><dd>Who played the most this week.</dd></div>
</dl>

Want a ping when people are on? Take **@SMP Ping** in **#roles**: the bot pings it in #monkey-smp when three or
more players are online.
