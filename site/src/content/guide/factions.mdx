A faction is a party of up to four players under its own banner. Once you join one you stay in it for the season;
leaving needs an admin's approval.

- **Claim land:** stand in a chunk and type `/oclaims claim`. Outsiders can't break, build or open anything in your
  claim. `/oclaims about` shows how many chunks you have left.
- **Who can use your land.** Each player owns the chunks they claim, and sets who may use them. By default your
  faction mates have full access: they can build, break and open chests there. You can change that (see below).
- **Allies** don't get into your claims unless you let them in. Alliances are fine; a group of more than four that
  always plays together is a confederation and breaks rule 32.
- **War and pacts.** Your faction's leader can declare war on another faction: until Sunday at 7 PM Pacific, kills
  between the two count twice in the weekly standings. Or offer a pact: no war between you until one side breaks
  it (two pacts at most). Neither changes what claims protect or what the rules allow.
- **Trading** is by hand, items for items: there's no money, shop or auction house. Lying about a trade is a scam
  and breaks the rules.

## Setting permissions on your land

Open the claims menu with the **Open Parties and Claims Menu** key (pick a key in Options, Controls), then
**My Player Config**. Each change applies to the chunks you claimed, not to your faction mates' chunks.

- **Full access** decides who can do everything in your claims: nobody, your party, your allies, everyone, or a
  player group you made.
- **Player groups** are your own lists of players, for example a "trusted" group with two of your faction mates.
  Make one under **Player Groups**, then choose it for any setting.
- **One kind of block at a time.** Chests, barrels, shulker boxes, doors, beds, furnaces, anvils, crops and more
  each have their own setting. For example, set full access to nobody and doors and beds to your party: your
  faction mates can come in and sleep, but they can't open your chests or break anything.
- **Different rules for different chunks.** A sub-config is a second set of these settings. Make one (a "vault",
  say), switch to it, and the chunks you claim next use it. Keep a locked vault and an open farm side by side.

Locking your own chests doesn't make insiding legal or illegal: rule 40 is the same either way. It just makes it
harder.
