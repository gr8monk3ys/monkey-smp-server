Get a pass and the modpack first ([how to join](/#join)). Then:

- **Talk to the Guide** at spawn: right-click it with an empty hand. It has the short rules, how to find a faction,
  and buttons for this week's challenges, the faction standings and your wardrobe. The **Town Crier** next to it
  has this week's Saturday event and next week's.
- **You're safe for 48 hours** after your first join: other players can't hurt you. Attacking someone ends it
  early, and so does `/trigger end_grace`. Grace is for settling in, not for scouting or carrying loot for a
  faction.
- **Spawn is safe.** Nobody can fight in the 7 &times; 7 chunks around it, and nobody but admins can build within
  48 blocks of it. PvP is on everywhere else.
- **Dying** drops your things where you died, as in plain Minecraft.
- **Voice chat** works by distance: press <kbd>V</kbd> for its settings. <kbd>M</kbd> opens the world map, and
  the <ConfigLink to="MAP_URL">live map</ConfigLink> shows the world in your browser (not where players are).

## On launch night

Season I opens on <LaunchTime template="{date} at {pacific} ({eastern})" />. For the first hour PvP, the Nether and
the End are closed; then PvP and the Nether open. The End opens on the night the Town Crier announces. Netherite
armor stays off until staff say so (Netherite tools and weapons are fine). Be online for 10 minutes on launch
weekend and you keep the Founder's Circlet skin.
