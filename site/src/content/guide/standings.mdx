## What the standings are

Each week every faction gets a rank, from the points it earned. The table is this week so far, which the
server updates about once an hour, and the last week's final table once there is one. The week closes on Sunday at
7 PM Pacific. The final table goes up in the Discord then, and a new week starts.

In the game, `/trigger standings` shows the board in chat from anywhere.

## How points work

- **Cup, up to 40.** Faction events add Cup points all season. The faction with the most Cup points gets 40.
- **Land, up to 20.** Chunks its members have claimed.
- **Strength, up to 20.** Its members' Strength levels added up.
- **Kills, up to 20.** PvP kills by its members this week, not counting the event arena. In a war, kills against
  the enemy count twice.
- **Bounties, +5 each.** For every bounty one of its members claimed this week.
- **Outposts, +1 an hour, up to 20.** For each whole hour it held an outpost this week while another faction was
  online.

For the first four, the faction in front gets the full number and the others get a share in proportion: a faction
with half the leader's land gets 10 of the 20. Bounties and outposts are added on top. A tie on points goes to the
faction with more Cup points, then more kills this week. Still level, they share a rank.

## Who is listed

Factions only: a name, a rank and points. Players aren't ranked on their own, and a player outside a faction isn't
listed. Faction names are chosen in the game. A name staff have hidden shows as "A faction".
