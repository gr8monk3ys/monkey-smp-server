## Supply drops

Chat gives the coordinates and a boss bar counts down 10 minutes. Then chests of food, iron, arrows and the like
land out in the newest land, away from every claim, and vanish 20 minutes later. The usual rules apply out there.

</Live>

<Live pack="monkey-omens">

## Omens

- **Blood Moon:** a night on Hard, with tougher mobs that drop a little extra.
- **Meteor:** a richer supply drop, with 5 minutes' warning.
- **Truce Hour:** no PvP anywhere for an hour.
- **Hunted:** the player on the longest kill streak gets a bounty for 30 minutes.

</Live>

## Standings<Live pack="monkey-outposts"> and outposts</Live>

</Live>

<Live pack="monkey-standings">

Every faction gets a rank each week from three things: event wins (the Cup), its members' Strength and its
kills. Bounties<Live pack="monkey-outposts"> and outposts</Live> add points on top. The table and the full scoring are on
[Weekly standings](/guide/standings).

</Live>

<Live pack="monkey-outposts">

Four **outposts** stand in the newest land, one each to the north, east, south and west. Stand in one with your
faction, and nobody else's, for 60 seconds to take it. Each hour you hold it while another faction is online is a
point, up to 20 a week. `/trigger outposts` shows where they are and who holds them.

</Live>

<Live pack={["monkey-firsts", "monkey-wardrobe"]}>

## Firsts and skins

</Live>

<Live pack="monkey-firsts">

The first player to reach each of 14 milestones (the first diamond, the first into the Nether, the first to slay
the dragon and so on) is announced to everyone and keeps it all season: `/trigger firsts`.

</Live>

<Live pack="monkey-wardrobe">

Skins for swords and helmets unlock as you play, come to events, finish challenges and stay on. They only change how
an item looks, never what it does: `/trigger wardrobe`.

</Live>
