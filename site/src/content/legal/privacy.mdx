Monkey SMP is a Minecraft server run by two people, Diego A. Scaturchio and Lorenzo D. Scaturchio, from
California, USA. "We" on this page means the two of us and the staff who help moderate. We don't sell what we
record, we don't show ads and we don't build profiles of players.

## This website

The site has no accounts and no forms, and it sets no cookies.

- **Hosting.** Vercel serves the pages. Like any web host, it sees your IP address and your browser's details
  when you load a page.
- **Page counts.** We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits: which page, the referring site, the country, the
  browser and the device type. Vercel says it uses no cookies and doesn't follow a visitor from one day to the
  next or from one site to another.
- **Live numbers.** The server status and the standings load from the machine that runs the game server and the
  <ConfigLink to="MAP_URL">live map</ConfigLink>. That machine sees your IP address when your browser asks for
  them, and when you open the map.
- **Email.** If you <ConfigLink to="CONTACT_URL">write to us</ConfigLink>, we keep the message so we can answer
  it.

## The game server

When you join, the server records what a Minecraft server needs to run and what we need to enforce the
[rules](/guide/rules):

- your Minecraft username and account ID (UUID), which Mojang's login gives every server;
- your IP address, and when you join and leave;
- chat messages and the commands you type, in the server log. That includes private messages sent by command;
- what you do in the world: blocks placed and broken, containers opened and what moved in or out. An action log
  keeps these so staff can undo griefing and settle reports;
- your game state: inventory, position, statistics, days played, faction, claims, ranks and unlocks;
- warnings, mutes and bans, with the reason and the staff member.

**Voice chat** is proximity voice. The server passes it to the players near you. We don't record it.

Staff read logs when there is a report, a suspected cheat or a bug to fix. To catch ban evasion and second
accounts, staff can see when two accounts connect from the same address.

## The store

The store runs on Tebex. Tebex is the seller: it takes the payment and handles billing, tax, refunds and
chargebacks under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or bank details. Tebex shows us the order: your
Minecraft username, what you bought, the price and date, and the contact details you gave at checkout. We use
those to give you what you paid for and to answer payment questions.

## Discord

The Discord server is optional. Discord runs it under Discord's own privacy policy. Messages you post there are
visible to the other members, and our bots keep what moderation needs (for example a ban appeal). A bridge can
carry game chat into a Discord channel, so treat public game chat as public there too.

## What other people can see

- Your username, your skin, your public chat and your builds, to players on the server.
- The world on the <ConfigLink to="MAP_URL">live map</ConfigLink>: terrain, builds and claims. The map does not
  show where players are.
- Faction names and points on the [standings](/guide/standings).
- Season Wrapped: at the end of a season, a page of each player's numbers for the season. Ask and we leave your
  name off it.
- News posts about server events, which can name the players and factions involved.
- Screenshots and clips that we or other players take.

## Who else handles it

We share what we record only with the services that run Monkey SMP:

- **Vercel**: website hosting and page counts.
- **Our server host**: the rented machine the game server, the map and the logs live on.
- **Cloudflare**: stores the world backups. They are encrypted before they leave the server.
- **Tebex**: the store and payments.
- **Discord**: the community server and the game chat bridge.
- **Mojang and Microsoft**: your Minecraft account signs in through them, under their terms.

We will also hand over records if the law requires it, or to deal with a real threat to someone's safety.

## How long we keep it

- **World and player saves**: for the season, and after it as an archive of the world.
- **Backups**: every backup from the last 48 hours, then one a day for 14 days, one a week for 8 weeks and one a
  month for 6 months. After that they are deleted. Server logs are not in the backups.
- **Server logs and the action log**: for as long as we need them to handle reports, bans and appeals, then they
  are deleted.
- **Ban records**: for as long as the ban stands.
- **Emails**: for as long as the matter is open, and a reasonable time after.
- **Orders**: Tebex keeps payment records for as long as the law tells it to.

## Your choices

<ConfigLink to="CONTACT_URL">Email us</ConfigLink> from an address we can tie to your account, or ask a staff
member in the game, and we will:

- tell you what we hold about your account and send you a copy;
- correct something that is wrong;
- delete your player data and your lines in the logs. Two limits: backups age out on the schedule above instead
  of being edited, and we keep a ban record while the ban stands;
- leave your name off the standings and Season Wrapped.

We answer within 30 days. You have these choices wherever you live. If the law where you live gives you more
rights over your data (for example in California, the EU or the UK), we honor them, and we will never treat you
worse for using them.

## Children

Monkey SMP is for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly record data about a child under 13. If you
believe a child under 13 is playing, tell us; we will remove the account from the whitelist and delete its
data. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to buy anything.
