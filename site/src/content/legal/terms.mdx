## Getting on the server

The server is whitelisted. A Server Pass puts your account on the whitelist for the time you paid for. A pass
is access for a period of time. It is not ownership of anything, and it is tied to the Minecraft account you
bought it for.

The server holds a limited number of players at once. When it is full there is a waitlist, and nobody can buy a
place ahead of it.

## Seasons and resets

The server runs in seasons of about 12 weeks. At the end of a season the world, items and progress can be
reset. The [news](/changes) says what carries over. A rollback or a restore from a backup, after a crash or an
exploit, can also undo recent progress. None of that is grounds for a refund.
